# The Contributor Ecosystem — How HiveAGI Sustains Itself

> This is the answer to "why would anyone contribute their human perspective
> to an open network?" Because they own it, they choose what to share, and
> the commercial value flows back to them.

---

## The timing — why now

AI glasses are about to go mainstream. Meta, Brilliant Labs, and others are
scaling hardware that captures first-person human experience continuously.

When that explosion happens, two things will be true:

1. **Everyone with glasses becomes a potential human-perspective contributor.**
2. **Big tech will capture that data into centralized servers — the same way
   they captured social media, search, and browsing.**

HiveAGI exists so that when the glasses moment arrives, there's an alternative:
a network where contributors **own their data, choose what to share, and
receive value back** — not a platform that extracts for free.

The infrastructure we're building now (Circles A-G) is the preparation. The
glasses hardware is the catalyst. The contributor ecosystem is the fuel.

---

## The three layers of the ecosystem

### Layer 1: Each contributor's ocean (private, local, owned)

Every contributor has their own ocean — their vault, their learned
preferences, their captured perspective. It's private by default. It's their
asset. A blogger's taste model belongs to the blogger. A guide's destination
knowledge belongs to the guide.

### Layer 2: The constellation (open, shared, growing)

The constellation forms when individual oceans contribute their excess — the
tags they choose to share. It's not raw experience. It's **structured human
judgment** — compact, like language. Anyone is welcome: a tourism guide in
Xi'an, a food blogger in Chengdu, a real-estate photographer in Sydney.

### Layer 3: Commercial value (sustains the ecosystem)

Goldman Forge builds commercial products using the open infrastructure and
contributor preference signals. Revenue flows back: funds Labs research,
supports active contributors, maintains the IPFS network, develops the mobile
app and glasses SDK.

---

## The cycle

```
CONTRIBUTOR captures → owns data → chooses to share tags (opt-in)
  → CONSTELLATION grows → convergence signal emerges
  → COMMERCIAL value created → revenue flows back
  → CONTRIBUTOR supported → captures more → cycle continues
```

**This is not a donation model.** Contributors invest their perspective into a
network that creates commercial value, and that value flows back.

---

## The big tech contrast

| | Big Tech AI | HiveAGI |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Who owns your data? | The platform | You (the contributor) |
| Who decides what's shared? | The platform | You (explicit consent, every time) |
| Who profits? | Shareholders | Contributors + the network |
| Privacy | "Trust us" | Code-enforced gates + human-controlled layers |
| When glasses arrive | Capture into their server | Your ocean, your choice |

# 貢獻者生態系統 — HiveAGI 的可持續運作模式

> 這是「為什麼有人願意將人類視角貢獻至開放網絡」的答案：因為他們擁有這些數據，他們選擇分享什麼，而商業價值會回流給他們。

---

## 時機 — 為什麼是現在

AI 眼鏡即將進入主流市場。Meta、Brilliant Labs 等公司正大規模擴展能夠持續捕捉第一人稱人類體驗的硬件設備。

當這一爆發性時刻到來時，將出現兩個事實：

1. **每一位佩戴眼鏡的用戶都將成為潛在的人類視角貢獻者。**
2. **大型科技公司將把這些數據收集到中心化伺服器中 — 正如他們此前收集社交媒體、搜索和瀏覽數據一樣。**

HiveAGI 的存在，是為了在眼鏡時代到來之際提供一個替代方案：一個讓貢獻者**擁有自己的數據、自主決定分享內容、並獲得價值回報**的網絡 — 而非一個免費索取數據的平台。

我們現在構建的基礎設施（Circles A-G）是準備工作。眼鏡硬件是催化劑。貢獻者生態系統是燃料。

---

## 生態系統的三層架構

### 第一層：每位貢獻者的海洋（私有、本地、自有）

每位貢獻者都擁有自己的海洋 — 他們的知識庫、已學習的偏好模型、所捕捉的視角數據。默認為私有。這是他們的資產。博客作者的品味模型屬於博客作者；導遊的目的地知識屬於導遊。

### 第二層：星座網絡（開放、共享、持續成長）

當個人海洋貢獻出多餘的部分 — 即他們選擇分享的標籤 — 星座網絡便隨之形成。這不是原始體驗數據，而是**結構化的人類判斷** — 精簡如語言。任何人都可以參與：西安的旅遊導遊、成都的美食博客、悉尼的房地產攝影師。

### 第三層：商業價值（維持生態系統運作）

Goldman Forge 利用開放基礎設施和貢獻者的偏好信號構建商業產品。收入回流至：資助 Labs 研究、支持活躍貢獻者、維護 IPFS 網絡、開發手機應用及眼鏡 SDK。

---

## 循環機制

```
貢獻者捕捉 → 擁有數據 → 選擇分享標籤（自願參與）
  → 星座網絡成長 → 匯聚信號出現
  → 商業價值創造 → 收入回流
  → 貢獻者獲得支持 → 捕捉更多 → 循環持續
```

**這不是捐贈模式。** 貢獻者將自身的視角投入一個創造商業價值的網絡，而該價值會回流。貢獻越多，網絡越有價值，所獲支持越大。

## 當眼鏡時代到來

```
新眼鏡用戶問：
  「我的視角數據去了哪裡？」

大型科技公司回答：
  「去了我們的伺服器。我們用它來改進 AI。你在條款第 47 段已同意。」

HiveAGI 回答：
  「存入你本地的知識庫。你的手負責處理。你擁有它。
   由你決定分享什麼。若你分享，網絡隨之成長，你將獲得支持。
   若你不分享，它將永遠屬於你。」
```

基礎設施（Circles A-G）是準備。眼鏡硬件是催化劑。貢獻者生態系統是燃料。循環之所以能自我維持，是因為每一位參與者 — 貢獻者、網絡、商業方 — 所獲得的價值均大於其所投入。
