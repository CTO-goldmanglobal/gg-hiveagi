# Script — Vertical (9:16, ~55s, Mobile)

> **Use:** Website hero (mobile), Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts
> **Resolution:** 1080×1920
> **VO:** warm Australian-English, calm, lower register
> **Music:** light guzheng/erhu underscore, ambient bed, ducks under VO

---

## SHOT 1 — HOOK · 0:00–0:06 (6s)

**Visual:** Slow push-in on the Great Wall winding over a misty ridge at dawn. Vertical crop keeps the wall leading up into frame.

**VO:**
> "China doesn't reveal itself all at once.
> It unfolds — over twelve days, five cities, and five thousand years."

**On-screen text (lower third, small):**
> LEGENDS OF CHINA · 12 DAYS

**Music:** single guzheng note, lets ring.

## SHOT 4 — XI'AN / WARRIORS (EMOTIONAL PEAK) · 0:22–0:32 (10s)

**Visual:** Slow pan across the Terracotta Army ranks. Hold on a single warrior's face. This shot gets the most screen time.

> every face different. Every face waiting."

**On-screen text:** *XI'AN · TERRACOTTA WARRIORS*

**Music:** **drops to near-silence** for the face reveal. Let it breathe.

## Vertical-specific notes

- All wides must be **cropped to 9:16**, not letterboxed. Plan the crop in shot selection — a Great Wall horizontal pan becomes a vertical push-in.
- Lower-thirds sit at **~70% frame height** (bottom third), small enough not to obscure the shot.
- The Terracotta Warrior face (Shot 4) should fill at least 60% of the vertical frame — the crop is the whole point.
- Text is **sans-serif, white, with a subtle drop shadow** — readable over any footage. No busy backgrounds behind text.
