## 1. Objective

Two promotional videos for the **Legends of China: Warriors Through Time — 12 Days** tour, matching the cinematic tone of the reference, each under 60 seconds:

| Version | Aspect | Use | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Vertical** | 9:16 (1080×1920) | Mobile — website hero, Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts | ~55s |
| **Landscape** | 16:9 (1920×1080) | Desktop — YouTube, website embed, Facebook | ~55s |

## 2. Tone & Brand Voice

Calm, confident, trustworthy. Never shouty, never "salesey." We are the expert guide who has done this a thousand times — the customer can relax because we have everything handled.

- **Pacing:** unhurried. Let each shot breathe ~3–4s.
- **Voiceover:** warm Australian-English narration, measured tempo, lower register. Female or male both work — pick whoever reads as "trusted local expert," not "announcer."
- **Music:** light Chinese-instrumental underscore (guzheng / erhu / dizi over a soft ambient bed). Never overwhelming the VO. Drops to near-silence under the Terracotta Warriors reveal.
- **On-screen text:** minimal. Tour name, price anchor ("from A$1,499"), the call to action. Day/place labels optional, small, lower-third.

## 3. Narrative Arc (both versions)

The tour is **"a passage through time, culture and civilisation."** The video should feel like that passage:

```
1. HOOK (0–6s)      — A single iconic image + the promise.
2. JOURNEY (6–45s)  — The arc of the trip, west-to-east, old-to-new:
                      Beijing (imperial) → Great Wall → Xi'an (ancient)
                      → water towns (serene) → Shanghai (modern)
3. TRUST (45–52s)   — What's included. The practical reassurance.
4. CTA (52–55s)     — Tour name, price, where to book.
```

## 4. The Visit Points (scripted in order)

The script sequences the **headline** visit points from the itinerary. Lesser stops (jade museum, TCM centre, train transfers) are implied visually, not narrated — they would dilute a 55-second cut.

| # | City | Visit Point | Story Beat |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Beijing | **Tiananmen Square + Forbidden City** | The imperial heart — "where every story begins" |
| 2 | Beijing | **Great Wall of China** | The legend — scale, endurance, the moment that sells the trip |
| 3 | Xi'an | **Terracotta Warriors** | The reveal — 8,000 faces, each one unique. The emotional peak. |
| 4 | Wuxi / Suzhou | **Lihu Park / Lingering Garden** | The breath — serene water-town beauty, contrast to the warriors |
| 5 | Hangzhou | **West Lake / Longjing Tea Village** | The taste — culture you can feel and drink |
| 6 | Shanghai | **Yu Garden + skyline** | The modern face — 400-year garden against the Bund skyline |

## 6. What this brief is NOT

- It is **not** a finished video. It is the blueprint a human editor (or the videogen pipeline, once real clips exist) cuts against.
- It does **not** include rendered audio. Music and VO are specified so they can be sourced/recorded, not generated here.
- It is **not** the tour page copy — that lives on the website. This is the video that *sells* that page.

## 7. Provenance & PII (Hive rules apply)

Any footage shot by ECH staff or customers must clear the **PII gate** — bystander faces and plates blurred before the cut. The videogen pipeline enforces this in code; manual edits must match the same standard.
