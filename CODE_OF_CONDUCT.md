# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Project Hive.AGI is building a human-perspective knowledge network. The people who make it — contributors, researchers, reviewers, and maintainers — come from different countries, disciplines, and levels of experience. We commit to making participation in this project a rigorous, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, and to handling the human-perspective data we work with (GPS-tagged, sometimes image-based) with the privacy care it deserves.

---

## Expected behavior

Across every Hive.AGI space, we ask that you:

- **Assume good faith.** Most people are trying to help. Read charitably and ask before accusing.
- **Be respectful and patient**, especially with contributors writing in a second language — this project runs in English and Traditional Chinese (`docs/zh-HK/`).
- **Critique ideas, not people.** Disagree with the design, the numbers, or the approach — not the person behind them.
- **Be rigorous, not hypey.** Back claims with evidence: cite specs, name models, show the reproduction. "Revolutionary" and "game-changing" are treated as code smells here.
- **Treat privacy as a first-class concern.** The project handles GPS-tagged, sometimes image-based human-perspective data. Never paste another person's identifying information into an issue, PR, or discussion.
- **Respect the maintainer's call.** A rejection is not a slight. We hold a high bar and document why.

---

## Unacceptable behavior

The following are not tolerated in any project space:

- Harassment, personal attacks, threats, or intimidation.
- Discrimination on the basis of race, ethnicity, nationality, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, religion, age, disability, or any other protected characteristic.
- Doxxing, or publishing — or threatening to publish — another person's private information.
- Trolling, derailing, or sustained disruption of discussion.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or imagery.
- Any other conduct a reasonable person would consider hostile or unwelcoming.

---

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for upholding this Code and applying consequences fairly and consistently. Consequences escalate with severity and recurrence:

1. **Warning** — private or public, for first or minor violations.
2. **Temporary ban** — from the project's spaces, for repeated or serious violations.
3. **Permanent ban** — for severe, deliberate, or repeated harm.

Maintainers may skip steps when the harm is serious. Threats, the deliberate publication of others' PII, or coordinated harassment will usually result in an immediate ban.

---

## Scope

This Code applies in all Hive.AGI spaces: GitHub issues, pull requests, Discussions, email, and the project Discord when it opens. It also applies whenever an individual is representing the project or its community in other spaces — for example, a maintainer speaking at an event, or a contributor posting under a project handle.

---

## Enforcement

Report violations privately to **<cto@goldmanglobal.com.au>**.

- All reports are reviewed and investigated discreetly by maintainers.
- The reporter's identity is kept confidential and shared only with those who need it to act.
- We aim to acknowledge reports within **72 hours**.
- **Retaliation against anyone who reports a Code violation is itself a violation** and will be treated as such.

If you believe a maintainer has violated this Code, report it to the same address — it will be handled outside that maintainer's direct line of influence.
