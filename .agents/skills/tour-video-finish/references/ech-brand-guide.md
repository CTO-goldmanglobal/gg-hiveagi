# ECH Brand Guide — End Card Reference

> Extracted from the corporate design guide. Read when making branding
> decisions for the end card, subtitles, or any ECH visual overlay.

## Brand idea
**Explore China with confidence.**
Trusted · Established · Warm · Premium · Clear · Authentic · Australian-facing · China-specialist

## Colour system

| Token | Hex | Use |
|:---|:---|:---|
| China Red | `#C8202F` | CTA, price, buttons, small accents. **5–10% of layout max.** |
| Deep Charcoal | `#171717` | Headings, navigation, important text |
| Warm White | `#FAF8F4` | Backgrounds (warm, not clinical grey) |
| Heritage Gold | `#B68A45` | CTS heritage, premium indicators. Sparingly. |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Pure white when photography requires it |
| Soft Grey | `#F1F1EF` | Secondary backgrounds |
| Muted Text | `#666666` | Supporting copy |

**Never combine red + gold + Chinese ornamentation everywhere.** That turns premium into souvenir-shop.

## Colour hierarchy
A frame should feel: **70% white/neutral · 20% photography · 7% charcoal · 3% red/gold accents**

## Typography
- **Primary:** Inter (body, UI, pricing, buttons)
- **Editorial:** DM Serif Display (large headings only — "Discover China")
- Body size: 17–18px (travel audience needs readable text)
- **No faux-Chinese display fonts.**

## End card principles
- **Calm, not aggressive.** No countdown timers, no "BOOK NOW!!!"
- **90% content / 10% brand.** The end card is trust + CTA, not a billboard.
- **Confident voice:** "We specialise in China." Not "we hope to make your dream possible."
- **Transparent:** "Here's what's included."
- The logo should be small enough that the footage still breathes.

## What to avoid
- Dragons, excessive lanterns, bright red backgrounds everywhere
- Fake calligraphy, gold gradients, clip-art pandas
- Generic "Asian" patterns, overdecorated borders
- China should come from **photography and destination storytelling**, not decoration
