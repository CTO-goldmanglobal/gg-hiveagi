# Music Direction — Legends of China Warriors

> Read when sourcing, selecting, or mixing music for the tour video.

## What we want

**Light Chinese-instrumental underscore** over a soft ambient bed.
Traditional timbres (guzheng, erhu, dizi) sitting on a modern, spacious pad.
Reads as "China" instantly without sounding like a period drama or tourist cliché.

## Reference cues

| Cue | Sounds like | Where in film |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Guzheng + ambient pad | Plucked zither over sustained drone. Spacious. | Opening hook (Shot 1) |
| Erhu enters | Two-stringed bowed fiddle, lyrical, slightly melancholic | Beijing (Shots 2–3) |
| Swells on hero shots | Crescendo — pad + light percussion | Great Wall (Shot 3), Warriors lead-in (Shot 4) |
| Drop to near-silence | Everything pulls back except faint pad | Terracotta face reveal (Shot 4) |
| Lighter return | Guzheng returns, brighter, major-leaning | Water towns / tea (Shots 5–6) |
| Resolve chord | Clean, confident landing | CTA outro (Shot 8) |

## Sourcing

| Source | Cost | Notes |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Artlist / Epidemic Sound | Subscription | Search: "china cinematic", "guzheng ambient", "erhu cinematic". **Check license covers ads.** |
| PremiumBeat / AudioJungle | Per-track | One-time sync license. Search same terms. |
| Commission | $300–$1500 | Composer writes to picture. Best result, highest cost. |
| AI (Suno/Udio) | Low cost, disclose | Workable for a bed, risks generic output. Not for hero track. |

## What to avoid
- Loud percussion/drums — kills the calm
- Western orchestral swells — reads "movie trailer"
- Stock "Asian" pentatonic kazoos — tourist cliché
- Vocal tracks — lyrics fight the VO

## Mix levels

| Element | Target |
|:---|:---|
| Voiceover | **-16 LUFS** (loud, clear, always on top) |
| Music bed | **-23 to -25 LUFS** (ducked under VO; +3dB in silent sections) |
| Final mix | **-14 LUFS integrated** (YouTube/Instagram spec) |

**Music must duck under VO.** Sidechain compression on the music bus keyed to VO,
or automate dips manually at each VO entry.
